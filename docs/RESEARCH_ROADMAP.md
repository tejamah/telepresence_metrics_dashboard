# Research Roadmap

## Paper Tracks

### Embodiment Prediction Models
Predict agency, ownership, presence, and immersion collapse from multimodal telemetry.

### Cognitive Telepresence
Model cognitive overload, attention drift, stress escalation, and teleoperation instability.

### Adaptive Reality Orchestration
Evaluate whether dynamic haptic, rendering, compression, and robot-control policies preserve embodiment.

### Emotional Synchronization
Measure collaboration trust, team cognitive load, interpersonal synchrony, and affective presence.

### Human Digital Twins
Learn personalized adaptation profiles, stress triggers, and recovery strategies for each operator.
